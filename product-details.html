Product Details

Precious Liquid Soap is formulated with a unique blend of cleaning agents that effectively remove dirt, grease, and stains while being gentle on your skin. Perfect for everyday use in households, offices, and commercial establishments.

Our liquid soap produces rich, long-lasting foam that provides excellent cleaning power with less product usage, making it economical and efficient.

Product Type Liquid Soap
Available Sizes 500ml, 1L, 5L
Scent Fresh Floral
pH Level Neutral (7.0)
Shelf Life 24 months

How to Use

  1. For hand washing: Apply a small amount to wet hands and lather well.
  2. For dishes: Add a few drops to warm water and wash as usual.
  3. For laundry: Use 50ml per load for hand washing or machine washing.
  4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
